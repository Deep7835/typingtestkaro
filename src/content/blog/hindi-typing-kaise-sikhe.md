Hindi typing seekhna mushkil lagta hai, kyunki keyboard par sirf English letters likhe hote hain. Lekin sach yeh hai ki agar aap sahi order mein seekhein, to 2 se 3 mahine mein aap aaram se 25 WPM tak pahunch sakte hain, jo zyadatar state exams ke liye kaafi hota hai.

Is guide mein hum step-by-step dekhenge ki Hindi typing kaise sikhe - bilkul beginner se lekar exam level tak. Language simple rakhi hai taaki pehli baar seekhne wale bhi aaram se samajh sakein.

## Step 1 - Sabse pehle layout chuniye

Hindi typing ke do main tareeke hain, aur shuru mein hi ek chunna zaroori hai.

| Layout | Kise chunna chahiye | Kahan kaam aata hai |
|---|---|---|
| **Mangal (Inscript)** | Naye beginners, central exams, office ka kaam | Unicode hai, har jagah chalta hai - website, email, mobile |
| **Krutidev / Remington GAIL** | UP, Rajasthan, MP, Bihar ke state exams ki taiyari | Purana typewriter wala layout, kai state exams mein accept hota hai |

Agar aapko pata hai ki aapka target exam sirf Krutidev ya Remington leta hai, to wahi seekhiye. Agar abhi decide nahi hai, to **Inscript** zyada safe choice hai kyunki yeh logical hai aur har jagah kaam aata hai.

Dono layouts ka detail comparison hamare Krutidev vs Mangal Inscript vs Remington wale article mein bhi diya gaya hai.

> Ek baar layout chun liya to beech mein mat badliye. Do layouts ek saath seekhne se ungliyan confuse hoti hain aur speed kabhi nahi banti.

## Step 2 - Sahi baithne ka tareeka aur finger position

Typing sirf ungliyon ka kaam nahi hai. Galat posture se aap 15 minute mein thak jaate hain.

- Kursi aisi ho ki kohni (elbow) lagbhag 90 degree par mude.
- Screen aankhon ke level par ya thoda neeche ho.
- Kalaai (wrist) keyboard par tikaaye nahi, halka upar rakhiye.
- Pair zameen par seedhe rakhiye.

Ab ungliyan **home row** par rakhiye. Left hand ki ungliyan A, S, D, F par aur right hand ki J, K, L, ; par. F aur J par chhota sa ubhaar (bump) hota hai - usi se bina dekhe position milti hai. Home row ka detail aap hamare article [home row keys and touch typing](/blog/home-row-keys-touch-typing/) mein dekh sakte hain.

## Step 3 - Home row ke Hindi akshar yaad kijiye

Pehle hafte sirf home row par focus kijiye. Har layout mein home row par alag akshar hote hain.

### Inscript (Mangal) home row

- Left hand: A = ो, S = े, D = ् (halant), F = ि
- Right hand: J = र, K = क, L = त, ; = च
- G = ु aur H = प (index fingers se)

### Remington GAIL (Krutidev) home row

- F = ि (yeh consonant se *pehle* type hota hai)
- D = क, K = ा, J = र, L = स
- Baaki keys course mein step by step aati hain.

Roz 15 se 20 minute sirf in keys ke combinations type kijiye - jaise कर, रक, कि, तर. Aankh screen par rakhiye, keyboard par nahi.

## Step 4 - Top row aur bottom row

Jab home row bina dekhe chalne lage (lagbhag 4 se 5 din mein), tab top row aur phir bottom row add kijiye. Ek din mein 3 se 4 nayi keys se zyada mat seekhiye.

Inscript mein top row par zyadatar matras hain - E = ा, R = ी, T = ू, W = ै, Q = ौ - aur consonants jaise U = ह, I = ग, O = द, P = ज. Shift dabane par kai keys ka "mahaapraan" roop aata hai, jaise K = क aur Shift+K = ख.

Hamare [Hindi Mangal Inscript course](/courses/hindi-mangal-inscript/) aur Remington GAIL course mein har row ke liye alag lessons hain, jo is kaam ko aasan bana dete hain.

## Step 5 - Matra, aadhe akshar aur sanyukt akshar

Hindi typing mein asli mehnat yahin lagti hai. English mein 26 letters hain, Hindi mein matras aur conjuncts ki wajah se combinations bahut zyada hain.

### Matra practice

Ek consonant lekar saari matras ke saath likhiye: क का कि की कु कू के कै को कौ कं. Phir yahi ग, त, म, स ke saath kijiye. Yeh boring lagta hai, lekin isse ungliyon ko matra keys yaad ho jaati hain.

### Aadhe akshar (half letters)

- **Inscript** mein aadha akshar banane ke liye consonant ke baad halant (D key) dabaiye: स + ् = स्. Phir agla akshar: स्त, स्व, न्द.
- **Remington** mein kai aadhe akshar ki alag keys hoti hain, unhe alag se yaad karna padta hai.

### Sanyukt akshar

क्ष, त्र, ज्ञ, श्र - yeh exam passages mein baar baar aate hain. Inko alag list bana kar roz 5 minute practice kijiye. Shabd jaise क्षेत्र, प्रशासन, राष्ट्र, विज्ञान, श्रमिक type kijiye.

> Galti hone par ghabraiye nahi. Shuruaat mein accuracy par dhyan dijiye, speed apne aap aayegi. 95% accuracy ke bina speed ka koi fayda nahi.

## Step 6 - Shabd aur vakya ki practice

Jab saari keys yaad ho jaayein, tab common Hindi shabdon par aaiye: है, और, के, में, की, से, को, था, एक, लिए, सरकार, भारत, विकास. Yeh shabd har passage mein aate hain, inhe itna practice kijiye ki bina soche type ho jaayein.

Uske baad chhote vakya aur phir paragraph. Is stage par aap hamare [Hindi typing online tool](/tools/hindi-typing-online/) mein free typing bhi kar sakte hain - jaise koi news paragraph dekh kar type karna.

## Step 7 - Timed test aur speed badhana

Ab asli exam jaisi practice ka samay hai. [Hindi typing test](/typing-test/hindi/) par roz timed test dijiye.

| Stage | Test ki lambai | Target speed | Target accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hafta 1-2 | 2 minute | 8-12 WPM | 90%+ |
| Hafta 3-4 | 5 minute | 12-18 WPM | 93%+ |
| Hafta 5-6 | 10 minute | 18-22 WPM | 95%+ |
| Hafta 7-8 | 10-15 minute | 25+ WPM | 95%+ |

Yeh targets ek aam guide hain. Kuch log jaldi pahunchte hain, kuch ko thoda zyada samay lagta hai - dono normal hai.

Har test ke baad apni galtiyan dekhiye. Jo shabd baar baar galat ho raha hai, use 10 baar type kijiye. Speed badhane ke aur tips ke liye hamara article [typing speed kaise badhaye](/blog/how-to-increase-typing-speed/) padhiye.

## Beginners ki aam galtiyan

- **Keyboard dekh kar type karna** - shuru mein dheere chalega, lekin bina dekhe hi type kijiye.
- **Ek hi ungli se kaam chalana** - har key ki apni ungli fixed hai, use follow kijiye.
- **Roz practice na karna** - hafte mein ek din 3 ghante se behtar hai roz 45 minute.
- **Sirf 1 minute ke test dena** - exam 10 se 15 minute ka hota hai, stamina bhi banani padti hai.
- **Layout badalte rehna** - ek layout mein pakke ho jaiye, phir sochiye.

## Rozana ka practice routine (45 minute)

- 5 minute: home row aur weak keys ka warm-up
- 10 minute: matra aur sanyukt akshar drill
- 10 minute: common shabdon ki practice
- 15 minute: ek timed test
- 5 minute: galtiyon ko dobara type karna aur speed note karna

Is routine ko 6 se 8 hafte lagataar follow kijiye. Zyadatar students is samay mein exam level speed ke kareeb pahunch jaate hain.

## FAQs

### Hindi typing sikhne mein kitna time lagta hai?

Roz 45 minute se 1 ghanta practice karne par zyadatar log 6 se 8 hafte mein 20 se 25 WPM tak pahunch jaate hain. Yeh aapki pehle ki typing aur regular practice par depend karta hai.

### Hindi typing ke liye kaunsa layout best hai?

Naye beginners ke liye Mangal Inscript aasan aur future-proof hai. Agar aapka target state exam Krutidev ya Remington leta hai, to Remington GAIL seekhna behtar rahega.

### Kya bina English typing jaane Hindi typing seekh sakte hain?

Haan, seekh sakte hain. Lekin agar English touch typing aati hai to finger position pehle se pata hoti hai, isliye Hindi thoda jaldi aati hai.

### Mobile par Hindi typing practice kar sakte hain?

Mobile par Hindi likhna alag skill hai. Exam computer keyboard par hota hai, isliye practice bhi physical keyboard par hi kijiye.

### Government exam ke liye kitni Hindi typing speed chahiye?

Kai state exams mein typically 25 WPM Hindi maangi jaati hai, aur kuch jagah kam ya zyada bhi hoti hai. Apne exam ki latest official notification zaroor check kijiye.

Shuruaat aaj hi kijiye - hamare [Mangal Inscript typing test](/typing-test/hindi-mangal-inscript/) par ek 2 minute ka test dijiye aur apni starting speed note kar lijiye.
